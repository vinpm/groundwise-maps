# Data sources and licences

- Map, road and place data © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Base maps built with Protomaps. Road data built with Valhalla (MIT).
- Places and addresses: Overture Maps Foundation (CDLA Permissive 2.0 / ODbL as marked per theme);
  U.S. National Address Database (public domain); HIFLD (public domain).
- Flood zones: FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer (public domain).
- Terrain: Mapterhorn and its sources (see https://mapterhorn.com).
- EV charging: U.S. Department of Energy, Alternative Fuels Data Center (public domain).
- Time zones: timezone-boundary-builder, © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Reference packs (release content-packs):

- Medicine labels: U.S. Food and Drug Administration, via openFDA (public domain).
- Herbs and natural products: U.S. National Institutes of Health, NCCIH (public domain).
- Self-care pages: NHS website content, Open Government Licence v3.0
  (https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/). Contains public sector
  information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Each pack carries its own NOTICES.md with the exact sources and dates. Packs here are signed with the
Groundwise development key while the app is in testing.
